German invoice rules for AI agents: mandatory fields and the e-invoice timeline 2025–2028

An agent that bills customers of a German business has to meet two sets of rules: the content of the invoice and, increasingly, its format.

Mandatory content (§ 14 (4) UStG)

A full invoice must contain, among other things:

  • name and address of the supplier and of the customer
  • the supplier's tax number or VAT ID
  • issue date and a sequential invoice number
  • quantity and type of goods or services, and the date of supply
  • the net amount, the tax rate and the tax amount, or a note on why no VAT is charged

Source: § 14 UStG. Small businesses under § 19 UStG (prior-year turnover up to €25,000 and current year up to €100,000) charge no VAT but must say so on the invoice (§ 34a UStDV).

E-invoice timeline for domestic B2B

Supplies made inWhat is allowed
2025 and 2026Every business must be able to receive e-invoices. Paper or other formats may still be issued.
2027Paper or other formats only if the issuer's prior-year turnover was at most €800,000.
from 2028Structured e-invoice (European standard EN 16931) for everyone.

Exceptions: invoices up to €250 (§ 33 UStDV) and invoices from small businesses under § 19 may always be sent in other formats. Transition rules: § 27 (38) UStG.
